UITM KUALA TERENGGANU - PUBLICATION PROCEDURES

Types of Publications:
- Journal Articles (ISI/Scopus)
- Conference Proceedings
- Books and Book Chapters
- Technical Reports

Publication Process:
1. Prepare manuscript according to target journal guidelines
2. Submit manuscript to Publication Unit for review
3. Receive feedback and make revisions
4. Format manuscript according to journal requirements
5. Submit to selected journal
6. Track publication status

Support Services:
- Proofreading and editing
- Formatting assistance
- Journal selection guidance
- Publication funding information
