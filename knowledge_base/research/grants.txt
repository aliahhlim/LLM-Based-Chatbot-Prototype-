UITM KUALA TERENGGANU - RESEARCH GRANT APPLICATION

Eligibility:
- Open to all permanent lecturers at UiTM Kuala Terengganu
- Must have completed at least 1 year of service
- No outstanding research obligations

Application Process:
1. Submit application form (available at RMU office)
2. Include research proposal (maximum 10 pages)
3. Provide budget breakdown
4. Attach CV of all researchers involved

Important Dates:
- Application opens: January 15
- Submission deadline: March 15
- Results announced: May 30
